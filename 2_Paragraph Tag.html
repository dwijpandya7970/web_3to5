Technology has become an essential part of modern life, influencing the way we communicate, learn, work, and entertain ourselves. From smartphones and computers to artificial intelligence and the internet, technological developments have made many tasks faster and more convenient. Students can access educational resources from anywhere, businesses can communicate with customers across the world, and people can stay connected with friends and family regardless of distance. However, technology should be used responsibly because excessive dependence on it can affect productivity, privacy, and social interaction. Therefore, maintaining a balance between technology and everyday life is important to make the best use of its benefits.
