Gujarati Traditional Recipes

1. Dhokla

Dhokla is one of the most popular Gujarati snacks. It is soft, light and delicious. Dhokla is prepared using fermented gram flour or rice and urad dal batter.

Main Ingredients: Gram flour, curd, salt, turmeric, green chilli, ginger and fruit salt.

2. Thepla

Thepla is a famous Gujarati flatbread made mainly with wheat flour and fresh fenugreek leaves. It is commonly eaten for breakfast or as a travel food.

Main Ingredients: Wheat flour, methi leaves, curd, turmeric, chilli powder, salt and oil.

3. Undhiyu

Undhiyu is a traditional Gujarati vegetable dish. It is especially popular during Uttarayan and winter. The dish is prepared with different seasonal vegetables, spices and fenugreek dumplings.

Main Ingredients: Surti papdi, potatoes, brinjal, raw banana, green peas, spices and methi muthia.
