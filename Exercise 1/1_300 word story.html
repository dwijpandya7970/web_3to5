The Old Lighthouse

Once upon a time, there was a small village near the sea. At the edge of the village stood an old lighthouse that had guided ships safely for many years. The lighthouse keeper was an old man named Thomas. He lived alone at the top of the tower and made sure that the light was always shining.

One stormy evening, the sky became dark and the sea grew wild. Strong winds shook the windows of the lighthouse. Thomas climbed the stairs to check the lamp, but suddenly the light went out. He tried to repair it, but one important part was broken. Without the light, ships could lose their way.

Thomas looked outside and saw a small fishing boat struggling against the huge waves. He knew that the fishermen were in danger. He quickly took an old lantern and climbed to the balcony. Holding the lantern high, he tried to guide the boat toward the safe side of the shore.

The fishermen saw the small light and followed it carefully. After a few minutes, the boat reached the shore safely. Everyone in the village came running to help them. The next morning, the villagers repaired the lighthouse together.

Thomas smiled as the lighthouse began shining again. He realized that even a small light can make a big difference in the darkest night. From that day onward, the villagers never forgot the courage of their old lighthouse keeper.

The End
